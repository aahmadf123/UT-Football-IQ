Remaining gaps:

These do not supply independently qualified designed-rollout context or a release-location observation packet. Passing outcomes and concepts require their own reviewed event/context lineage.
- **Pressure-free and pressure-to-sack summaries:** the underlying explicit pressure-coverage durations exist, but a dedicated censor-aware specialist population/summary producer is separate work; it must not average censored durations as complete values.
- **Expected outcomes:** `stage_expected_outcomes.py` and `pipeline/expected_outcomes/` now provide routed extraction/inference/candidate infrastructure. This audit found no evidence of approved external held-out Toledo accuracy. The fresh cross-service transport test correctly suppressed all four predictions because field mapping was not analytics-safe.

## Page 14 — OL, gaps and protection

`position_evidence.py:original_front` preserves original named roles; `oline_observations.py:measured_lanes` subtracts measured body extents and explicit complete defender occupancy at the LOS. `stage_oline.py:run` emits pre-snap splits and geometric spacing; `run_observations.py:observed_interactions` consumes explicit paired engagement/chip events.

Remaining gaps:

- **Geometry now implemented:** `specialist_geometry.py` supplies measured envelope dimensions/compression, original named lineman first-second displacement and signed pass-set depth from explicit pass-protection participation, snap/LOS/direction and supported tracks. `run_observations.py:observed_runs` samples actual entry lane width. The later `lane_windows.py` slice adds discrete measured lane transitions and explicit handoff/entry-aligned widths; its interval-censored clocks must retain the bounds between measured samples. Discrete snapshots do not establish continuous opening or successful lanes. The lane slice is separately reviewed by its owner/root.
- **Single/double teams, chips, combo release, pulls, penetration conceded, pressure by matchup, stunt exchange and shed events:** `stage_oline.py:_detect_double_teams`, `_find_release_frame`, `_detect_stunts`, `_compute_block_shed_timing` and `_detect_pursuit_cutoff` are proximity/path heuristics. They remain experimental hypotheses, not qualified interaction facts. `_compute_pass_set_depth` is a short-window motion measure, not proof of pass-protection assignment.
- **Staff authoring now reachable:** `frontend/src/app/clip-review/observed-evidence-fields.tsx` authors explicit pressure intervals/completeness, original snap role map, source-shot measured body extents and the complete observed defender population, plus engagement_start/end and chip_end/route_start exact linked endpoint contracts. `run_observations.py:observed_interactions` now requires the same known start/end shot; no across-cut duration is emitted. A TE chip state is still distinct from chip_end.

## Page 15 — RB, WR and TE

`run_observations.py:observed_runs` implements explicit carrier, signed gain, handoff-to-LOS, actual entry and qualified first-contact splits. `observed_position_products.py` now supplies receiver separation/paired paths and explicit TE usage transitions. Generic speed/distance/workload observations remain in `stage_metrics.py`, `metrics/kinematics.py` and `metrics/physical.py`.

Remaining gaps:

- **Reviewed RB cut/tackle evidence now implemented:** `observed_skill_outcomes.py` consumes explicit carrier_cut and tackle_outcome events joined to named possession/actors, emits supported cut locations and explicit made/missed/assist/forced-miss counts, and preserves signed observed YAC prefixes. Root's initial 19 reviewed skill cases were expanded to 22 after contradictory-reception hardening. This is deterministic reviewed-event plumbing, not a learned tackle/vision classifier. `stage_metrics.py:_count_broken_tackles`, `_detect_cutback_timing` and `_vision_decisiveness` remain legacy hypotheses and cannot establish vision or blocking success. Actual pass-protection outcomes still need explicit interaction/outcome definitions beyond participation and geometry.
- **WR/TE geometry now implemented:** `specialist_geometry.py` emits signed route depth, snap alignment/split relative to a role-qualified original C, and separately qualified receiver_release/route_break measured point packets. Point evidence can survive a later route loss; an arbitrary offense track cannot become the C. `stage_routes.py` still has heuristic route family/combination labels, which do not establish qualified football concepts or execution quality.
- **Reviewed pass relationships/YAC now implemented:** `observed_skill_outcomes.py` emits explicit targets, target/catch outcomes, contested target outcomes, staff-confirmed coverage responsibility and signed observed YAC from linked reception/terminal evidence. `observed-outcomes-fields.tsx` authors those exact reviewed inputs. Missing, ambiguous or contradictory observations do not become negative outcomes; a separately observed target may survive a missing result. These do not qualify automatic press-release/coverage/contact judgments or establish a validated observed-YAC population. Expected YAC infrastructure remains a separate model/evaluation boundary.
- **TE alignment subtypes:** inline/slot/backfield classification has no dedicated qualified observation producer. Usage-transition states are now explicit; chip-to-route delay still requires the distinct qualified chip_end and route_start pair in `observed_interactions`.
- **Independent denominators now implemented:** `observed_position_products.py` additionally emits rushes, targets, pass_protection, tackle_opportunities and returns, with role/team checks and no trajectory/end/outcome prerequisite. Existing routes/pass_rush/pursuit/coverage/blocking/kicking opportunities remain supported. Root API owns canonical-play/player/kind deduplication and player-rate qualification. Total snaps and successful-measurement counts cannot substitute for these explicit observations.
